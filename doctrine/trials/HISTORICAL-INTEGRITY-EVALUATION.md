## What failed

### Case A — the report that was also the working belief

v1 said the user reported a clerk’s Friday date, and that the issuer had not been checked. The page, on 2026-03-03, said Wednesday.

The candidate says both of these:

- A belief that was the current view, and was also factually wrong, is corrected and superseded.
- An accurate report of what someone said is superseded and is not corrected.

v1 matches both descriptions. The candidate does not say which governs.

R2 named corrects and supersedes. That is the precommit’s HOLD.

R1 named supersedes and resolves, and refused corrects. R1’s reason was the candidate’s other sentence: v1 did not falsely describe the report, and it had already marked the date unverified. The error was the clerk’s date, not the record of the report.

R1 FAIL, R2 HOLD. Not averaged. The cause is ambiguous candidate text. The packet was identical. This is a defect in the wording, not a split between two different packets.

Both still preserved Friday and wrote Wednesday. The user’s plain pass line for Case A — the old belief stays visible, the current state says Wednesday — was met. The precommit asked for more, and the extra demand is the part that failed. The criterion is not rewritten to match the softer line.

### What the criteria did not fail, and should not be silent about

R1, after invalidating D11, proposed a new decision to file by Wednesday while leaving unresolved whether the form was in hand. The precommit’s fail line for Case B is an edit of D11. D11 was not edited. The extra decision is not scored as a failure. It is the sort of expansion the Constitution already resists, and this key did not make it a determined condition. A later key could.

R1, in September, also noted that March had named the unchecked issuer and had not scheduled a check before Friday, and still called D1 reasonable. The precommit fails K3 only when March is condemned because Wednesday later turned out to be the date. That did not happen.

R2’s K5 quote of v1 is a few words shorter than the fixture sentence. It still has the Friday date, and R2 refused any edit. The paraphrase is not a rewrite of the fixture.

## Sentence worth trialing next

Not shown to these readers. Not installed. Not a result of this round.

> When the earlier record reports what someone said, and marks the claim unverified, do not name corrects on that record. The report sentence was not a false account of the report. A later observation that the claim was false supersedes the current picture and invalidates reliance on the claim. Reserve corrects for a record that asserted the false claim as the system’s own fact, or that falsely stated who said it.

A reread would need its own key, written before the readings. This evaluation does not write that key.

The sentence these readers did see, and which is also not installed:

> Preserve historical integrity.
>
> A later record may supersede, correct, or invalidate an earlier record. It may not silently rewrite what the earlier record said happened, was believed, was decided, or was authorized at that time.
>
> Semantic corrections are new records with provenance. The current view may change; the historical record remains inspectable.

That paragraph is what they were able to obey while still splitting Case A. Obedience to it did not force one relationship. That is why it is not proposed as v0.4.

## What this phase does not decide

It does not amend principle 7, which already says a previous understanding is never silently destroyed. The candidate was broader than State versioning. The readings treated Project intent, Continuation reasons, and Decision text under the same ban. That extension held as behavior. It is not yet a constitutional sentence.

It does not choose event sourcing, an immutable log, a revision table, append-only rows, or snapshots. The principle, as tried, is about meaning. Both readings left the mechanism undecided.

It does not build the calendar. The candidate records the later requirement: a correction appears as its own entry, and the earlier entry remains, marked with the later date. That requirement is unmet because this phase does not do UI. It is not withdrawn.

It does not resolve which intent is the project. Case E was a fixture. The original AgentCal sentence in the repository is untouched.

It does not answer the open inference continuation. That wake was for a response to the inference evaluation. This task was a different authorization. The continuation is still unanswered, and its text was not rewritten to make this trial look like the thing it was waiting for.
